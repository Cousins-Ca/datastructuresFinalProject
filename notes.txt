Use arrow keys to move left/right
Press 'Z' to jump
Press 'X' to flow
	When you flow midair, you wont be affected by gravity for 20 frames
Press 'C' to slide while on the ground, or dive while midair
	When hitting the ground from a dive, you will bounce high up
	Sliding increases your jump height

You have 3 ground speeds
	Bit gear - Default state, automatically return to it when standing still
	Byte gear - Activated by sliding, diving, and entering flow for a short time
	Kilo gear - Activated by being in flow for 40 frames
